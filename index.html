The Movement

The Explorers Campaign: Discover Nigeria

Nigeria is not just one story. It is 36 vibrant states, 250+ distinct ethnic cultures, ancient royal kingdoms, coastal lagoons, and mountain waterfalls waiting to be unlocked.

The Explorers Campaign is a nationwide cultural and travel initiative designed to connect people who want to explore with the events, tours, spots, and local businesses that define our heritage. Every state will have its own dedicated Explorer chapter: from Lagos Explorers and Osun Explorers to Kano, Ogun, and Cross River Explorers.

Our Story & Purpose

About The Africa Explorers

The digital gateway connecting people who want to explore with places, events, and experiences worth exploring.

Our Story

Travel information across Nigeria was fragmented across social media, WhatsApp groups, and word-of-mouth. We founded The Africa Explorers NG to build a single trusted ecosystem: connecting destination → event → tour → accommodation → dining → community.

Our Vision

To become the digital gateway through which the world discovers Nigeria and Africa. From Lagos and Osun to all 36 states, we answer: Where should I go? What is happening? Where can I stay? Who else is exploring?

How We Help Explorers

Every explorer carries one digital passport. In your Home State, you are an Explorer; when you travel to another state, you are a Visitor. We provide curated tours, verified safety, direct WhatsApp bookings, and member rates.
