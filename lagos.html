About Lagos State

Lagos is Nigeria's commercial capital and a melting pot of cultures, ideas and people. It's a city that never sleeps — where tradition meets modernity, and opportunities are endless. From the Lekki-Ikoyi axis to the historic shores of Badagry, Lagos offers a unique blend of history, entertainment, food, art and innovation.

Land Area 3,577 km²
Major Languages English, Yoruba, Pidgin
