About Osun State

Osun State is a place where nature, culture and spirituality come together. Home to the famous Osun-Osogbo Grove, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, the state is also known for its rich Yoruba traditions, warm people, delicious cuisine and vibrant arts. Whether you're drawn to history, nature, festivals or adventure, Osun has something special for you.

Land Area 9,251 km²
Major Language Yoruba, English
Best Time to Visit Nov – Feb (Dry season)
